The Double Deuce: A Short History
(found in the office file cabinet, in Garrett's own handwriting at the bottom)

Nobody remembers who ran this building before it was a bar. It was a feed store first,
everybody agrees on that much, though nobody agrees on when it stopped being one.

What's better documented: it opened its doors as the Double Deuce in 1962, and has been
a roadhouse ever since - some years rougher than others. There was a fire in the kitchen
back in '78 that closed the place for most of a summer, and a stretch in the mid-80s that
old regulars still bring up if you let them talk long enough.

Current capacity, per the last fire marshal inspection, is 295. Post it by the door.
Nobody wants a visit from the county over a headcount.

Live music most nights. Been that way since before I got here, and I don't plan on
changing it.

- G.
